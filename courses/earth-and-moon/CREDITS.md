# Earth & Moon credits

The course pairs an orthographic model of the Earth–Moon system with a synchronized observer view. Body sizes, distances and eclipse shadow widths are scaled for teaching. The seasonal model uses a circular orbit and a 23.44° axial tilt. Day lengths and pole shadows use geometric sunlight, without atmospheric refraction or terrain.

## Visuals, narration and typography

- Earth and Moon surfaces use the [planet textures from the three.js r160 examples](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160/examples/textures/planets). The cover, chapter illustrations, diagrams and films were rendered for this course.
- The prerecorded English and Mandarin narration was synthesized with the [Piper Lessac and Huayan voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices). The course distributes audio recordings.
- DM Sans, Instrument Serif and Noto CJK are bundled as font subsets. Their notices are included in [assets/fonts](assets/fonts/).
