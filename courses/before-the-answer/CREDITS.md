# Before the Answer credits

## Research and data

Based on Wes Gurnee, Nicholas Sofroniew and coauthors, *Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models*. Anthropic, Transformer Circuits Thread, July 6, 2026. [Read the paper](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html).

The course redraws selected measurements from the authors’ public figure data. The six experiments replay recorded conditions. The browser runs no language model. See the [figure-by-figure source inventory and measurement notes](DATA.md).

## Narration, films and artwork

The English and Mandarin explanatory scripts, translations and thirty motion-graphic scenes were made for this course. Narration was synthesized using Piper’s [English Lessac and Mandarin Huayan voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices). The production directory includes the finished narration and the film renderer. All caption times are tied to the corresponding recording.

The hero, cover and six chapter illustrations are original AI-generated editorial artwork. The glass plates and optical instruments illustrate the topic. Measured results are displayed separately in labeled charts and readouts.

The player bundles DM Sans and Instrument Serif under the SIL Open Font License. Copies accompany the [font files](assets/fonts/). Film rendering also uses the system’s Noto CJK and DejaVu fonts. The original paper, data and source materials retain their authorship and applicable terms.
